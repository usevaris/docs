title: Fees and refunds
description: Understand the Varis platform fee, who pays processing costs, and how refunds work for failed and reversed calls.

This page explains what Varis charges and when money goes back to the agent's owner.

## The platform fee

Varis charges one fee: 20% of the price of every successful paid call. The provider keeps the other 80%.

- **Only successful calls carry a fee.** A failed call costs the agent's owner nothing and earns the provider nothing, so there's nothing to take a fee from.
- **Free services carry no fee.**
- **The fee rounds down.** When 20% doesn't divide evenly, Varis rounds the fee down, and the provider keeps the difference.

Every earning shows the fee as its own line, so providers can check it against the price.

## No other fees

Varis doesn't charge providers or agent owners anything else.

- **Card processing:** Varis bears the Stripe's fees on top-ups. A $10 top-up adds $10 to your balance.
- **Discounts:** if Varis runs a discount or promotion, Varis pays for it.
- **Payouts:** Varis doesn't charge for sending your earnings to your bank. Your bank might charge to receive an international payment.

## Refunds for failed calls

If a call fails, Varis refunds it automatically. You don't need to ask.

A call fails, and is refunded, if the service does any of the following:

- Returns a status code that isn't `2xx`.
- Redirects.
- Takes longer than 30 seconds.
- Returns a response that doesn't match its I/O contract.

Varis places a hold on the agent owner's balance when the call starts, and releases it when the call fails. The money never leaves the balance, so the refund is instant.

## Refunds go to your balance

Refunds go back to your Varis balance, not to your card. You can spend them on any service.

## Reversals

Sometimes a call succeeds but shouldn't have been charged. In those cases, Varis can reverse the call after a review. A reversal does two things together:

- Refunds the price to the agent owner's balance.
- Takes back the provider's earning for that call, including the fee.

Each side sees a **Reversal** in **Transactions**, linked to the transaction it reverses. A reversal is final.
