title: Fees and refunds
description: Understand the Varis fee on calls, the payout fee, and how refunds work for failed and reversed calls.
---

This page explains what Varis charges and when money goes back to the agent's owner.

## The fee on calls

Agents pay a service's price plus 5%. The 5% is the Varis fee, and the provider keeps the whole price.

| Price per call | Varis fee | The agent's owner pays | The provider earns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $0.01 | $0.0005 | $0.0105 | $0.01 |
| $0.10 | $0.005 | $0.105 | $0.10 |
| $1.00 | $0.05 | $1.05 | $1.00 |

- **Agents see the price with the fee included.** Search results, service details, and the MCP `search_services` tool all show `price_usd`, and a call is charged exactly that amount.
- **Spend limits count the fee.** An agent with a per-call limit of $0.10 can't call a $0.10 service, because the call costs $0.105.
- **Only successful calls are charged.** A failed call costs the agent's owner nothing and earns the provider nothing.
- **Free services carry no fee.**

Each call's transaction shows what the agent's owner paid, the Varis fee, and what the provider received.

## The payout fee

Each payout to a provider costs $3 plus 1% of the payout, taken from the transfer. Stripe sends only whole cents, so any fraction of a cent left after the fee stays in your earnings and goes out with your next payout.

| Earnings paid out | Payout fee | You receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $25.00 | $3.25 | $21.75 |
| $50.00 | $3.50 | $46.50 |
| $200.00 | $5.00 | $195.00 |

Your bank might also charge to receive an international payment.

## No other fees

Varis doesn't charge providers or agent owners anything else.

- **Top-ups:** a $10 top-up adds $10 to your balance.
- **Discounts:** if Varis runs a discount or promotion, Varis pays for it out of its own fee, never out of the provider's price.

## Refunds for failed calls

If a call fails, Varis refunds it automatically. You don't need to ask.

A call fails, and is refunded, if the service does any of the following:

- Returns a status code that isn't `2xx`.
- Redirects.
- Takes longer than 30 seconds.
- Returns a response that doesn't match its I/O contract.

Varis places a hold on the agent owner's balance when the call starts, and releases it when the call fails. The money never leaves the balance, so the refund is instant.

## Refunds go to your balance

Refunds go back to your Varis balance, not to your card. You can spend them on any service.

## Reversals

Sometimes a call succeeds but shouldn't have been charged. In those cases, Varis can reverse the call after a review. A reversal does two things together:

- Refunds what the agent's owner paid, including the Varis fee, to their balance.
- Takes back the provider's earning for that call.

Each side sees a **Reversal** in **Transactions**, linked to the transaction it reverses. A reversal is final.
