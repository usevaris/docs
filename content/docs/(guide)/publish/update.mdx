---
title: Update a service
description: Change a published service's details, price, schemas, or endpoint, and disable or re-enable it.
---

You can change any field of a published service except its slug. Your changes apply to the next call that an agent makes.

## Update your service

<Tabs items={['Dashboard', 'CLI']}>
<Tab value="Dashboard">

1. In the [dashboard](https://usevaris.com/services), go to **Services**.
1. Click the service you want to change.
1. Click **Edit**.
1. Change the fields you want.
1. Click **Save changes**.

</Tab>
<Tab value="CLI">

1. Change the `define` call for your service.
1. If you changed the endpoint, deploy your server first.
1. From your project folder, run:

   ```bash
   varis publish
   ```

The CLI reports `updated` for each service you changed and `unchanged` for the rest. To publish only one service, pass its slug:

```bash
varis publish convert-currency
```

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Publishing replaces the whole service

When you run `varis publish`, Varis sets every field of the service to what's in your `define` call. A field you remove from `define` goes back to its default, or is removed from the service. For example:

- If you remove `instructions`, the service no longer has instructions.
- If you remove `method: "POST"`, the method goes back to `GET`.
- If you remove `price_cents`, the service becomes free.

Check your `define` call before you publish, so that you don't reset a field by mistake.

## Change the slug

You can't change a slug. If you publish with a new slug, Varis creates a second service and leaves the first one as it was.

To move to a new slug, publish the service under the new slug, and then disable the old service.

## Change the schemas or method

Agents read your input schema to build each call, so a change to the input schema or method changes what your endpoint receives. Deploy the endpoint that handles the new input before you publish the change.

A change to the output schema applies straight away. If your endpoint returns the old shape after you publish, Varis treats each call as failed and the agent isn't charged.

## Disable or re-enable a service

When you disable a service, agents can't find or call it. Calls already in progress finish.

<Tabs items={['Dashboard', 'CLI']}>
<Tab value="Dashboard">

1. In **Services**, click the service.
1. Click **Disable**, and then confirm.

To re-enable it, open the service and click **Re-enable**.

</Tab>
<Tab value="CLI">

1. Add `status: "disabled"` to the `define` call:

   ```ts
   varis.services.define<Input, Output>({
     slug: "convert-currency",
     // ...
     status: "disabled",
   });
   ```

1. Run `varis publish`.

To re-enable it, remove `status` or set it to `"published"`, and then run `varis publish`.

</Tab>
</Tabs>
<Callout type="warn">
If you disable a service in the dashboard and then run `varis publish` without `status: "disabled"`, the CLI publishes the service again. Keep the dashboard and your code in agreement.
</Callout>
