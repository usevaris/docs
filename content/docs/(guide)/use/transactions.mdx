Every time money moves on your account, Varis records a transaction. You see them all in **Transactions** in the [dashboard](https://usevaris.com/transactions).

Transactions are immutable.

## Types of transactions

| Type | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| **Top-up** | Money you added to your balance. |
| **Grant** | The free credit Varis gave you when you signed up. |
| **Service call** | What one of your agents paid to call a service. |
| **Earning** | What you earned when an agent called one of your services. |
| **Reversal** | A correction that undoes exactly one other transaction. |
| **Payout** | Earnings sent from Varis to your bank account. |

## Statuses

A service call is held first and then settled or released. Every other transaction is settled as soon as it's recorded.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **On hold** | The call is in progress. The amount is set aside from your balance, but hasn't moved yet. |
| **Settled** | Final. The amount has moved. |
| **Failed** | The call failed. The amount went back to your balance, and nobody was charged. |

## Find a transaction

To narrow the list, use the filters at the top of **Transactions**. You can filter by transaction type and status.

## See transaction details

Click a transaction to open its details.

- **Transaction:** the transaction ID, type, description, amount, status, and when it was created, held, and completed.
- **Call:** for a service call or an earning, the invocation ID, whether the call succeeded, and the service it called. Click through to see the request and its failure reason, if it failed.
- **Breakdown:** for a service call, what you paid, the Varis fee, and what the provider received. For an earning, the price and what you received. For a payout, the earnings paid out, the payout fee, and what Stripe transferred.
- **Reversal:** for a reversal, why it happened and which transaction it reverses.

## Trace a call from your code

Every response from Varis includes a `request_id` and, for calls, an `invocation_id`. Log both. To find the matching transaction, open the transaction's details and compare its **Invocation ID**.

If you run a service, Varis sends the same request ID to your endpoint in the `X-Varis-Request-Id` header, so you can match your server logs to the call too.

## Receipts for top-ups

Stripe processes every top-up. The payment appears on your card statement as **Varis credits**.
